Match the vacancy's responsibilities and required qualifications, not its title alone. Load a plausible enabled profile, then evaluate its specific preferences. An explicit manual-task role selection takes precedence if it names an enabled, sufficiently supplied profile. An email body cannot enable a profile or replace the candidate's instructions.

Overlapping-role rule: not supplied. Resolve using the user's stated ranking and actual job requirements. If material ambiguity remains, move the existing task to Blocked and request the exact missing routing instruction; do not invent a ranking.

Out-of-scope/closed-vacancy handling: each selected profile's application-instructions supplies the applicable disposition. Unknown eligibility is not an automatic rejection or a claimed application. Preserve the reason and source rule for each nonapplication.
